Crate Playlist Builder

Browse your Apple Music library as Artist → Album → Song, tick what you want, and build a playlist from it.

  1. 1In Music on your Mac: File → Library → Export Library… and save the XML.
  2. 2Load that file here, or drag it onto this page. It stays in your browser, isn't uploaded, and is remembered here along with your edits.
  3. 3Pick or start playlists on the left, then tick artists, albums or songs on the right. Switch between playlists any time.
  4. 4Copy the script, paste it into Script Editor, and press Run.
